I lead engineering teams, and lately I spend my evenings learning to build with LLM agents. I only really understand something once I have explained it to someone else, so this blog is where I explain it.

## What you will find here

Mostly agentic AI: notes from building agents myself, covering the loop, tools, memory, evals, and the many ways an agent breaks. My first Series, _Building an agent from scratch_, is where that starts.

Some leadership, too: what I am learning about leading engineers while the tools we use keep changing this fast.

And now and then music, which is how I switch off.

## How I write

Posts are short when the idea is short. I would rather publish something small and true than something long and polished. When I get something wrong, I update the Post and say what changed.

If something here is useful to you, or wrong, tell me on [GitHub](https://github.com/vfil) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vlad-filimon-60a14a39/).
