I lead engineering teams, and lately I have been spending my evenings learning how to build with LLM agents. The best way I know to learn something properly is to explain it to someone else, so this blog is where I do that.

## What you will find here

- **Agentic AI.** Notes from building agents myself: the loop, tools, memory, evals, and the many ways it breaks. My first Series, _Building an agent from scratch_, starts here.
- **Leadership.** What I am learning about leading engineers, especially now that the tools we use are changing so fast.
- **Music.** Occasionally. It is how I switch off.

## How I write

Posts are short when the idea is short. I would rather publish something small and true than something long and polished. When I get something wrong, I will update the Post and say what changed.

If something here is useful to you, or wrong, tell me on [GitHub](https://github.com/vfil) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vlad-filimon-60a14a39/).
