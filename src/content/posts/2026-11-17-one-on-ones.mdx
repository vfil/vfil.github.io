For years my one-on-ones were status updates with better coffee. These are the habits I dropped once I noticed.

> The 1:1 is their meeting, not yours. If you are doing most of the talking, it has become yours.

## What changed

| I used to                      | Now I                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| Ask "what are you working on?" | Read the tracker beforehand              |
| Bring my own agenda            | Let them bring theirs, and add mine last |
| Solve the problem in the room  | Ask what they have already tried         |

## The order I use now

1. Their topics, for as long as they need.
2. Anything I have noticed that they might not have.
3. One question about where they want to be in a year.

The third one is the one people remember.
