# vfil blog

A personal, learning-in-public blog about agentic AI, leadership and (occasionally) music.

## Language

### Content

**Post**:
A single published piece of writing, of any length, written as one Markdown/MDX file.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, blog (as a noun for one piece)

**Draft**:
A Post marked as not ready; visible while writing locally, never published.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, WIP

**Quiz**:
A multiple-choice question inside a Post. The reader answers once and immediately sees whether they were right, and why.
_Avoid_: Poll, test, exercise

### Series

**Series**:
An ordered sequence of Posts on one theme, with its own title, introduction and status; it is independent of Categories.
_Avoid_: Collection, course, saga, multi-part post

**Part**:
A Post's position (1, 2, 3…) within its Series; published Parts always run from 1 without gaps.
_Avoid_: Chapter, episode, installment

**Planned Part**:
A Part announced in a Series but not yet published: either a title listed in the Series, or a Draft that belongs to the Series. Readers see its title but cannot open it.
_Avoid_: Upcoming post, stub, placeholder post

**Ongoing** / **Complete**:
A Series' status: Ongoing while more Parts are expected, Complete once the author declares it finished.
_Avoid_: Active, open, closed, finished

### Appearance

**Mode**:
Whether the site is shown dark or light; follows the reader's system setting until they choose one.
_Avoid_: Theme, color scheme

**Palette**:
The site's colours, derived from three hues 120° apart plus a strength and a background tint.
_Avoid_: Theme, skin, color scheme

**Default Palette**:
The Palette the author chose; what every reader sees until they pick their own.
_Avoid_: Base theme, brand colours

**Custom Palette**:
A Palette a reader picked for themselves; it applies only in their own browser.
_Avoid_: User theme, personal theme

### Classification

**Category**:
One of a small, fixed set of subject areas (Agentic AI, Leadership, Music); every Post belongs to exactly one, and Categories form the site menu.
_Avoid_: Section, topic, channel

**Tag**:
A free-form keyword attached to a Post; a Post may have zero or more, and Tags may cut across Categories.
_Avoid_: Label, keyword, hashtag
